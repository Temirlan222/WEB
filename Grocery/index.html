About Us

Welcome to Lido Grocery Store at Uly Dala 31. Find everyday groceries, drinks and household essentials at Lido. The products available include food, drinks, snacks, sweets, home care products, fresh fruits and vegetables, meat products, and other non-food items. The benefit of the Lido market is that you can go shopping at any time of day because the store is operating around the clock 24/7.

Opening Hours

Lido Grocery Store is open 24/7, which makes it convenient for customers who need to buy groceries early in the morning, during the day, or late at night.

The store can be used both for quick purchases and for larger everyday shopping.

About This Website

Store information and photographs come from the team visit. Catalogue names and reference prices come from Galmart Astana listings recorded on 2 November 2026. Confirm current prices and availability with Lido. This site prepares a local shopping list, not an online order.
