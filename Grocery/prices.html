Product Prices

The content of this page represents a summary of costs for a number of goods that were available at Lido Grocery Store. In this case, the most frequently purchased categories were selected, allowing users the possibility of gauging approximately how much visitors have to spend on groceries. The goods presented in the table can be distinguished by their type. Prices may change during the week — always check the shelf price

Go directly to the price table or read information about the prices.

Price Information

Product pricing may fluctuate because shops receive new supplies, conduct sales activities, and change their product mix. Hence, the amount mentioned here is not fixed since the amounts given here are based on our observations at the time of our visit. Also, buyers should confirm with the current shelf price before buying any given product.

How We Collected the Prices

  1. We visited Lido Grocery Store in person.
  2. We selected products from several different categories.
  3. We checked the prices shown on the shelves.
  4. We recorded the information for this website.

This table is meant to provide visitors with a basic impression of daily prices of items in the store. It is not the entire range of items available in the store. More items can be discovered on the Products page.
