Product Variety

Household cleaning

Apart from packaged foods and household goods, fresh produce, particularly fruits and vegetables, is also sold in Lido on a daily basis.

Products such as apples, bananas, tomatoes, cucumbers and potatoes can be found among all the usual products sold in Lido. Lido has bottled water, juices, drinks and many other kinds of beverages.

Several goods are stored in refrigerators so as to keep them fresh. The wide variety of foods and beverages helps the customers to make their daily purchases easily.

Product Information

Lido offers everyday essentials and fresh products for its customers. The store also sells popular brands and some ready-to-eat products. Selected goods may sometimes be on promotion. Bottled H2O can be found in the beverages section.
