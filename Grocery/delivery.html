What to ask

  1. Ask whether delivery is available to your address.
  2. Name the products, package sizes and quantities you need.
  3. Confirm the total price, delivery charge and payment method.
  4. Agree on the address and expected delivery time.

For example, specify whether you need a loaf of bread, Barni, sausage, cheese, chips, soap or shampoo. Ask about stock before agreeing on substitutions. If delivery is unavailable, ask whether you can collect the items in person.
