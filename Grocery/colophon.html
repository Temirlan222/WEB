Your shop, at your pace

Explore 50 everyday groceries, save the items you like and put together a shopping list. Check the estimated total before you visit Lido at Uly Dala Avenue, 31.

The site helps you prepare. Call the shop to confirm current prices, stock and pickup or delivery arrangements.

About this student project

This student project describes Lido in Astana. Temirlan is responsible for the home and products pages. Murager contributed prices and delivery. The standalone Cart brings together the existing shopping-list features. Saparali Shalkar is responsible for contacts and this page. The seven HTML files belong in the same folder, with photographs inside images/.

The site uses HTML, Bootstrap, JavaScript and jQuery. Your shopping list stays in this browser. Forms prepare information to review before you call or visit the shop. Internet access is needed for the CDN libraries and external links.

How it works

Search by product or brand in English or Russian. Save favourites with the heart button, then use your cart to change quantities and download the list.

There is no online payment or automatic order submission. You stay in control of the next step.

Technical notes & sources

The telephone links open the calling app on your device. The example below shows the address used by the Call Lido buttons:

<a href="tel:+77479052505">
    Call Lido
</a>

Check each saved file with the W3C Nu Html Checker. A successful result reads Document checking completed. No errors or warnings to show.

Your list stays yours

Your cart and favourites are saved in this browser. The telephone link opens a calling app. The visitor can then call the shop to discuss delivery. The product request demo on the Products page does not send customer details anywhere.
